Probability Formulas

Here are some basic probability formulas that are frequently used in quantitative aptitude exams, along with a table for quick reference:

Formula Description
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) – P(A and B) Probability of the union of two events
P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B|A) = P(B) x P(A|B) Probability of the intersection of two events
P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B) Conditional probability
P(B|A) = P(A and B) / P(A) Conditional probability
P(A’) = 1 – P(A) Probability of the complementary event
P(A) + P(A’) = 1 Probability of the sample space
P(A and B) = 0 if A and B are mutually exclusive events Probability of two mutually exclusive events
P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B) if A and B are independent events Probability of two independent events
P(A and B) > P(A) if A and B are dependent events Probability of two dependent events
P(A) x P(B) ≤ P(A and B) ≤ min(P(A), P(B)) Inequality for the probability of intersection
P(A or B or C) = P(A) + P(B) + P© – P(A and B) – P(A and C) – P(B and C) + P(A and B and C) Probability of the union of three events
