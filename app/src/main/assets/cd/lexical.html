Lexical Analysis

The scanner groups characters into tokens, discards whitespace and comments, and enters identifiers into the symbol table.

TermMeans
Tokenthe category, such as identifier or operator
Lexemethe actual characters matched
Patternthe rule describing what matches

In sum = a + 10, the tokens are id, assign, id, plus, number — five tokens, five lexemes, and only three patterns.

Tokens form regular languages, so a finite automaton recognises them in linear time with no stack. That is precisely why lexing is separate from parsing.

The longest-match rule resolves ambiguity: given >=, the scanner returns one token, not > followed by =. Keywords beat identifiers on ties.
