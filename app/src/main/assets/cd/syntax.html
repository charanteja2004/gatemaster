Syntax Analysis

The parser checks that the token stream fits the grammar and builds a parse tree recording the derivation.

A context-free grammar is (V, T, P, S). Its productions have a single variable on the left, which is exactly what a stack can handle.

  • A parse tree shows every production applied; a syntax tree keeps only the operators and operands.
  • A leftmost derivation always expands the leftmost variable; a rightmost derivation the rightmost.
  • Top-down parsers trace a leftmost derivation; bottom-up parsers trace a rightmost one in reverse.

Nesting is why a regular grammar is not enough. Balanced brackets are the standard proof that the language of well-formed programs is not regular.
