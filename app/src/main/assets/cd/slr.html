SLR Parser

Build the LR(0) item sets, then decide reductions using the FOLLOW set of the left-hand side.

  1. Augment the grammar with S' → S.
  2. Compute the LR(0) canonical collection with closure and goto.
  3. For item A → α· with A ≠ S', put reduce in every column of FOLLOW(A).
  4. Shift entries come from goto on terminals.

FOLLOW is a global set, computed without regard to the state the parser is in. That coarseness is the whole weakness of SLR: it may place a reduce where the context forbids it, creating a conflict a finer lookahead would avoid.

Every SLR(1) grammar is LALR(1) and CLR(1). The reverse fails, which is what the classic three-way comparison questions turn on.
