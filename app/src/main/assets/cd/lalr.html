LALR Parser

Take the CLR automaton and merge states with identical cores, unioning their lookaheads.

Merging can create reduce-reduce conflicts but never shift-reduce conflicts, because shifts depend only on the core, which merging leaves untouched.

LALR gives nearly CLR's power with SLR's table size, which is why YACC and Bison generate LALR(1) parsers by default.

An LALR parser may perform some extra reductions before announcing an error, though it never shifts a symbol a CLR parser would reject.
