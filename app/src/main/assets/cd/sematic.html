Syntax-Directed Translation

A syntax-directed definition attaches attributes to grammar symbols and rules for computing them to productions.

AttributeComputed fromFlows
Synthesisedthe childrenupward
Inheritedthe parent or siblingsdownward and sideways

In E → E1 + T, the rule E.val = E1.val + T.val is synthesised. In D → T L, the rule L.type = T.type is inherited.

Synthesised attributes can be evaluated during a bottom-up parse, which is why S-attributed definitions fit LR parsing exactly.
