ALU and Data Path

The ALU performs the arithmetic and logic; its function inputs come from the control unit and its flags feed back into it.

  • Arithmetic: add, subtract, increment, compare.
  • Logic: AND, OR, XOR, NOT.
  • Shift: logical, arithmetic and rotate.

Subtraction reuses the adder: A − B is A + (2's complement of B), which is why hardware needs no separate subtractor, only an inverter and a carry-in of 1.
