RISC and CISC

RISCCISC
Instruction lengthfixedvariable
Instructionsfew, simplemany, complex
Memory operandsload and store onlymost instructions
Addressing modesfewmany
Controlhardwiredusually micro-programmed
Registersmanyfewer
CPIlow, near 1higher and variable

RISC moves complexity from the hardware to the compiler. Fixed length and load-store are not aesthetic choices — they are what makes a clean pipeline possible.

The distinction has blurred: modern x86 decodes complex instructions into RISC-like micro-operations internally, keeping the old interface over a new organisation.
