Issues in Computer Design

Architecture is what the programmer sees — the instruction set, registers and addressing modes. Organisation is how it is built — the datapath, control and memory hierarchy.

CPU time = instruction count × CPI × clock period

Every optimisation attacks one of those three terms, and usually worsens another. RISC lowers CPI and raises instruction count; CISC does the reverse.

Amdahl's law bounds the payoff: speeding up a fraction f of the work by s gives an overall speedup of 1 / ((1 − f) + f/s). Making a rare case fast is wasted effort.
