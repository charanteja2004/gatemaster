Carry Look-Ahead Adder

Compute all carries directly from the inputs instead of waiting for them to ripple.

G(i) = A(i)·B(i), P(i) = A(i) ⊕ B(i)

C(i+1) = G(i) + P(i)·C(i)

Expanding the recurrence gives every carry as a two-level function of the inputs, so addition takes constant gate delay rather than delay proportional to n.

The cost is fan-in and gate count, both growing quickly with width. Real adders use look-ahead in blocks of four and ripple between blocks.

Ripple carryO(n)
Carry look-aheadO(log n) in practice, O(1) for one block
