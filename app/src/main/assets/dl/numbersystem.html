160–9, A–F4 bits per digit
  1. Integer to another base: divide repeatedly, read remainders bottom-up.
  2. Fraction to another base: multiply repeatedly, read integer parts top-down.
  3. Binary to octal or hex: group bits in 3s or 4s from the binary point.

(1011.11)₂ = 8 + 2 + 1 + 0.5 + 0.25 = 11.75. (2C)₁₆ = 0010 1100 = (54)₈.

Group from the binary point outwards, not from the left. Grouping 101100 from the wrong end gives the wrong octal digits.
