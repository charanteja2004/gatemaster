n flip-flops count 0…2ⁿ−1; a mod-m counter needs n = ⌈log₂ m⌉ flip-flops

A mod-10 counter needs 4 flip-flops and resets when it detects 1010, leaving six unused states.

Ripple counters are cheap and slow; the accumulated delay caps the counting frequency and produces transient wrong values on the outputs.
