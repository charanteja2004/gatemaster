Parallel Adder and Subtractor

n full adders side by side add two n-bit numbers. The same circuit subtracts when the second operand is complemented.

Feed B through XOR gates controlled by a mode line M, and tie M to the carry-in. M = 0 gives A + B; M = 1 gives A + B' + 1, which is A − B in 2's complement.

Overflow is not the carry-out. For signed operands, overflow is the XOR of the carry into and out of the most significant bit.
