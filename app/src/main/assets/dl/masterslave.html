Master-Slave Flip-Flop

Two latches in series on opposite clock phases: the master samples while the clock is high, the slave copies while it is low.

Only one latch is ever transparent, so the output cannot feed back into the input within a clock period. That is what eliminates the race-around condition of a level-triggered JK.

The effect is edge triggering built from level-sensitive parts, which is why the arrangement predates true edge-triggered designs.
