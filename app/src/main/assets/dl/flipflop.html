JK removes the SR forbidden state by turning it into a toggle, which is what makes counters possible. D is what registers are built from.

Latch against flip-flop

A latch is level-sensitive and transparent while enabled; a flip-flop is edge-triggered and samples at one instant. That distinction decides whether a circuit is racy.

Setup and hold times bound how close to the clock edge the input may change. Violating them gives metastability, not merely a wrong value.
