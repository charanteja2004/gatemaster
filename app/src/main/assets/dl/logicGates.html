NAND and NOR are universal: each alone can build NOT, AND and OR, and therefore any function. AND and OR together cannot, because neither can complement.

  • NOT from NAND: tie the inputs together.
  • AND from NAND: a NAND followed by a NAND used as NOT.
  • OR from NAND: invert both inputs, then NAND.

XOR is the parity and inequality gate: it detects a difference on two inputs, and odd parity on more.
