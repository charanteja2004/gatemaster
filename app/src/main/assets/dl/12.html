1's and 2's Complement

  • 1's complement: invert every bit.
  • 2's complement: invert every bit and add 1.
  • Shortcut: copy from the right up to and including the first 1, invert the rest.

8-bit: 00010110 is 22; its 2's complement is 11101010 = −22. 11111011 is −5.

2's complement wins because addition and subtraction use the same adder and there is a single zero. The asymmetric range — one more negative than positive — follows from that.

Signed overflow is detected when the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of it.
