Deadlock in Databases

Two transactions each hold a lock the other needs. The same circular wait as in operating systems, resolved by aborting one of them.

SchemeRulePreempts?
Wait-dieolder waits, younger diesnon-preemptive
Wound-waitolder wounds younger, younger waitspreemptive

Both use timestamps and both guarantee no cycles, because waiting always runs in one direction of age. A restarted transaction keeps its original timestamp, which is what prevents starvation.

Detection uses a wait-for graph: a cycle means deadlock, and the victim is usually the transaction with the least work done.
