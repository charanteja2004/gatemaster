Relational Algebra

OperationSymbolDoes
Selectσpicks rows matching a condition

Renameρrenames a relation or attributes

Those six are the primitives; join, intersection and division are derived. A natural join is a product, a selection on the common attributes, and a projection.

Projection removes duplicates because a relation is a set. SQL's SELECT keeps them unless you write DISTINCT — the most common mismatch between the theory and the language.
