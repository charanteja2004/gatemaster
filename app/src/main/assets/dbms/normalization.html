Normalisation

Decompose relations to remove redundancy, so that a fact is stored once and cannot become inconsistent.

  • Lossless join: the decomposition can be joined back exactly. Non-negotiable.
  • Dependency preservation: every original dependency is still enforceable on one table. Desirable.

A binary decomposition of R into R1 and R2 is lossless exactly when R1 ∩ R2 determines R1 or determines R2. That test appears in nearly every normalisation question.

Normalising past 3NF can cost dependency preservation. BCNF is always achievable losslessly, but not always with dependencies preserved.
