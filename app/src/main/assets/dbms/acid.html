ACID Properties

PropertyGuarantees
Atomicityall of the transaction or none of it
Consistencyconstraints hold before and after
Isolationconcurrent transactions do not see each other's partial work
Durabilitya committed change survives a crash

Atomicity is delivered by the undo log, durability by the redo log and forced writes at commit, and isolation by locking or timestamps.
