Minimisation of ER Diagrams

Fewer tables mean fewer joins. The rules for collapsing relationships into existing tables are the practical part of ER design.

  • A 1:N relationship never needs its own table.
  • A 1:1 relationship with total participation on one side can be merged into that side.
  • M:N and n-ary relationships always need their own table.
  • A multivalued attribute always becomes its own table.

The minimum number of tables for a set of entities is the standard exam calculation. Count entities, then add one per M:N relationship and one per multivalued attribute.
