Engineering Mathematics: revision sheet

Engineering Mathematics is about 13 marks in the CS paper, spread over discrete mathematics, linear algebra, calculus and probability.

Set theory and relations

  • A relation is reflexive, symmetric and transitive ⟹ equivalence relation ⟹ it partitions the set.
  • Reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive ⟹ partial order.
  • Functions from a set of size m to one of size n: nᵐ. Injections need n ≥ m.

Counting

C(n, r) = n! / (r!(n − r)!); P(n, r) = n! / (n − r)!

Inclusion-exclusion: |A ∪ B| = |A| + |B| − |A ∩ B|

Pigeonhole: n items in k boxes force some box to hold at least ⌈n/k⌉.

Graph theory

  • Σ degrees = 2|E|.
  • A tree on n vertices has n − 1 edges; a complete graph has n(n−1)/2.
  • Euler circuit ⟺ connected and every degree even.
  • A simple planar graph satisfies V − E + F = 2 and E ≤ 3V − 6.

Linear algebra

  • Ax = b has a unique solution ⟺ det(A) ≠ 0 ⟺ rank = n.
  • Σ eigenvalues = trace; Π eigenvalues = determinant.
  • A symmetric real matrix has real eigenvalues and orthogonal eigenvectors.
  • rank + nullity = number of columns.

Calculus

  • Rolle and the mean value theorem need continuity on [a,b] and differentiability on (a,b).
  • L'Hôpital applies only to 0/0 and ∞/∞.
  • A maximum needs f'(x) = 0 and f''(x) < 0.
