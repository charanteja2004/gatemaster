Comparison sorting cannot beat Ω(n log n): a decision tree with n! leaves has height Θ(n log n). Counting, radix and bucket sort escape it by not comparing.

Quick sort's worst case is sorted input with a first or last pivot. Greedy fails 0/1 knapsack. Dijkstra fails on negative edges. Those three are the most examined traps in this subject.
