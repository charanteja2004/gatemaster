Remainders

For powers, reduce the base modulo the divisor first, then find the cycle length of the remainders.

7¹⁰⁰ mod 5: 7 ≡ 2, and powers of 2 cycle 2, 4, 3, 1 with period 4. Since 100 is a multiple of 4, the remainder is 1.

A number leaving remainder r with several divisors is r more than a common multiple. The least such number is LCM + r — for 12, 15 and 20 with r = 3 that is 63.
