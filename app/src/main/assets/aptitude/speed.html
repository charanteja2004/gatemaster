Speed, Time and Distance

Distance = speed × time

km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18. m/s to km/h: multiply by 18/5

Average speed over equal distances = 2xy/(x + y)

40 km/h out and 60 km/h back gives 2×40×60/100 = 48 km/h, not 50. The slower leg takes longer, so it weighs more.

Trains

  • Crossing a pole: distance is the train's length.
  • Crossing a platform or bridge: distance is train length + platform length.
  • Two trains in opposite directions: speeds add. Same direction: speeds subtract.

A 150 m train at 90 km/h (25 m/s) crossing a 300 m bridge covers 450 m in 18 s.

Boats

Downstream = boat + stream; upstream = boat − stream
