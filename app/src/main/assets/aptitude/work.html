Work and Wages

If A finishes in a days, A's rate is 1/a of the work per day

Together: 1/a + 1/b = 1/t, so t = ab/(a + b)

12 days and 18 days together: 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36, so 36/5 = 7.2 days.

Man-days

M₁ × D₁ × H₁ / W₁ = M₂ × D₂ × H₂ / W₂

12 men for 20 days is 240 man-days. After 8 days, 96 are done and 144 remain; with 8 men left that is 18 more days, so 26 in total.

Work as a total of man-days handles every joining-and-leaving variation. Rates as fractions handle every together-and-alone variation. Pick whichever matches the question.

Negative work is the same arithmetic: someone undoing work has a negative rate, which is exactly how an outlet pipe is modelled.
