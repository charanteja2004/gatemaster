Reading Comprehension

  1. Read the passage once for the main idea, not for detail.
  2. Read the question and identify what it asks: fact, inference, tone or main idea.
  3. Return to the passage for the relevant lines.
  4. Choose the option the passage supports, not the one that is merely true.

An inference must follow from the passage alone. Outside knowledge, however correct, is the commonest wrong answer in this section.

  • Extreme words — always, never, all — are usually wrong in inference options.
  • Options that restate a sentence verbatim are often traps for the main idea.
  • Tone questions turn on adjectives and adverbs, not on the subject matter.
