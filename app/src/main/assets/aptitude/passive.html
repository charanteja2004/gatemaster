Active and Passive Voice

Active: subject + verb + object ⟶ Passive: object + be + past participle + by subject

TenseActivePassive
Simple presentwritesis written
Present continuousis writingis being written
Simple pastwrotewas written
Present perfecthas writtenhas been written
Futurewill writewill be written

The tense must survive the change. 'They are building a bridge' becomes 'A bridge is being built', not 'is built'.

The agent is dropped when it is unknown or unimportant, which is why 'Someone has stolen my bicycle' becomes simply 'My bicycle has been stolen'.
