Profit and Loss

Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100; Loss% = (CP − SP)/CP × 100

SP = CP × (100 + profit%)/100

SP after discount = MP × (100 − discount%)/100

Marked 40% above cost with a 25% discount: 140 × 0.75 = 105, so a 5% profit on a cost of 100.

Profit percentage is always on cost price and discount is always on marked price. Mixing the two bases is the standard trap.

Selling two articles at the same price, one at x% profit and one at x% loss, always gives an overall loss of x²/100 percent — never break-even.
