Grammar Basics

Grammar rules is the set of guidelines given in order to properly use words so that meaningful sentences can be prepared. Various rules are designed so that better usage of English language can be done of effective conversation.


All the rules are mentioned below:
1.

Sentence structure: A sentence must have a subject and a predicate.
2. Parts of speech: Words are categorized into nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
3. Agreement: Subject and verb must agree in number.
4. Tense consistency: Verbs must be used consistently in the same tense.
5. Pronoun agreement: Pronouns must agree with the antecedent in gender and number.
6. Modifiers: Adjectives and adverbs must be placed correctly in sentences.
7. Parallelism: Ideas in a list or comparison must be expressed in parallel form.
8. Capitalization: The first word of a sentence and proper nouns must be capitalized.
9. Punctuation: Proper use of punctuation is crucial for clear and concise writing.
10. Spelling: Correct spelling is important for clarity and credibility.
11. Word choice: Precise word choice is essential for effective communication.
12. Tone: The tone of a piece of writing should match the purpose and audience.
