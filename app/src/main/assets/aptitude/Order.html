Ranking and Order

Total = position from left + position from right − 1

Position from right = total − position from left + 1

11th from the left and 15th from the right gives 11 + 15 − 1 = 25 in the row.

In a row of 40, the 12th from the left is the 29th from the right.

The −1 exists because the person is counted from both ends. Adding the two positions without it is the single most common error in this topic.

If the two counts overlap — the totals do not agree — the question is describing two different people, or people are being counted between rather than including.
