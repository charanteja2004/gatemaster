Pipes and Cisterns

Identical to work and time: a filling pipe has a positive rate and an emptying pipe a negative one.

Net rate = Σ filling rates − Σ emptying rates; time = 1 / net rate

Filling in 10 h against emptying in 15 h: 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30, so the tank fills in 30 hours.

A (20 min) and B (30 min) open together, A closed after 5 min: 5 × (1/20 + 1/30) = 5/12 filled, and B finishes the remaining 7/12 in 17.5 min — 22.5 min in total.

If the emptying rate exceeds the filling rate the tank never fills, and the question is testing whether you check the sign.
