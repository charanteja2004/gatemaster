Percentages

Successive changes multiply: a rise of x% then a fall of y% gives a net factor of (1 + x/100)(1 − y/100)

Up 20% then down 20% gives 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96, a net fall of 4%. The two do not cancel because the second percentage is taken on a larger base.

If A is x% more than B, then B is (100x)/(100 + x)% less than A

A is 25% more than B, so B is 2500/125 = 20% less than A, not 25%.

Price and consumption

Price rises by x% ⟹ consumption must fall by (100x)/(100 + x)% to keep expenditure fixed

Read which quantity the percentage is taken on. Nearly every wrong answer in this topic comes from using the wrong base.
