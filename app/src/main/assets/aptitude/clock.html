Clocks

  • The minute hand moves 6° per minute.
  • The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute, or 30° per hour.
  • The minute hand gains 5.5° per minute on the hour hand.

Angle = |30H − 5.5M| degrees, taking 360 − θ if the result exceeds 180

At 4:20 the angle is |120 − 110| = 10°, not 0 — the hour hand has already drifted a third of the way to 5.

  • The hands coincide 11 times in 12 hours, so 22 times a day.
  • They are opposite 11 times in 12 hours, and at right angles 22 times.

There is no coincidence between 11 and 12 o'clock — the 11 o'clock and 12 o'clock cases are the same event, which is why the count is 11 and not 12.
