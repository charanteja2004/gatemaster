Arithmetic Progression

AP: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d; Sₙ = n/2 × (2a + (n − 1)d) = n/2 × (first + last)

GP: aₙ = a·rⁿ⁻¹; Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1); S∞ = a/(1 − r) for |r| < 1

First term 5, common difference 3, twenty terms: S = 10 × (10 + 57) = 670.

  • Sum of the first n natural numbers: n(n+1)/2.
  • Sum of their squares: n(n+1)(2n+1)/6.
  • Sum of their cubes: [n(n+1)/2]².

Given two terms, subtract to find d: aₚ − a_q = (p − q)d. That one step solves most AP questions without finding the first term at all.
