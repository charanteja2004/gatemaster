Data Interpretation

A table, bar chart or pie chart with questions about totals, percentages and growth. The arithmetic is easy; the reading is where marks are lost.

  1. Read the units and the scale before anything else.
  2. Note whether values are absolute or percentages.
  3. Compute totals once and reuse them.
  4. Approximate when the options are far apart.

Percentage change = (new − old)/old × 100

Sales 40, 50, 45, 60 over four years: the growth from the first to the last is 20/40 = 50%, while the largest year-on-year rise is 15/45 = 33.3%.

In a pie chart each 1% is 3.6°. Questions frequently give degrees and expect a value, or the reverse.

The largest percentage increase and the largest absolute increase need not be the same year. Check which one is asked.
