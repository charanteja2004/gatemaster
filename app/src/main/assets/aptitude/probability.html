Probability

P(E) = favourable outcomes / total outcomes, 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)

Independent events: P(A ∩ B) = P(A)·P(B)

Conditional: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

Standard counts

  • Two dice: 36 outcomes; a sum of 7 occurs 6 ways, so P = 1/6.
  • Three coins: 8 outcomes; exactly two heads occurs 3 ways, so P = 3/8.
  • Drawing without replacement: the denominator shrinks each draw.

4 red and 6 blue, two drawn without replacement: (4/10) × (3/9) = 2/15 ≈ 0.13.

Mutually exclusive and independent are different. Mutually exclusive events with non-zero probability are never independent — if one happens the other cannot.
