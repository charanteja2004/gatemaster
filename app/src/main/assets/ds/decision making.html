Decision Making in C

  • if / else if / else for arbitrary conditions.
  • switch for equality against integer constants only.
  • The conditional operator ?: as an expression rather than a statement.

A switch case without break falls through to the next case. Fall-through is occasionally intended and far more often a bug, and exam questions rely on it.

In C any non-zero value is true, including negative numbers and pointers. Comparing to 1 rather than to non-zero is a common source of wrong answers.
