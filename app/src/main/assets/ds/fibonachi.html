Fibonacci Series

F(0) = 0, F(1) = 1, F(n) = F(n−1) + F(n−2)

MethodTimeSpace
Naive recursionΘ(φⁿ) ≈ Θ(1.618ⁿ)O(n)
MemoisedΘ(n)O(n)
Iterative, two variablesΘ(n)O(1)
Matrix powerΘ(log n)O(1)

The naive version recomputes F(n−2) twice, F(n−3) three times and so on — the call tree has about F(n) leaves. It is the standard motivation for dynamic programming.
