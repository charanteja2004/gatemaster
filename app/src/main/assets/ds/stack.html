Stack

A stack is a last-in-first-out collection with push, pop and top, all O(1).

  • Function call management and recursion.
  • Expression conversion and evaluation.
  • Undo, backtracking, and iterative DFS.
  • Balanced bracket checking.

For n distinct items pushed in order, the number of valid push-pop sequences is the Catalan number C(n) = (2n)! / (n!·(n+1)!). C(3) = 5 and C(4) = 14 appear regularly.

Implemented on an array, push and pop are O(1) until a resize is needed; on a linked list they are always O(1) with a pointer per node.
