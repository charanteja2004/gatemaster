Infix to Postfix Conversion

Postfix needs no brackets and no precedence rules, which is why machines evaluate it.

  1. Operands go straight to the output.
  2. An operator pops operators of greater or equal precedence, then is pushed.
  3. ( is pushed; ) pops until the matching ( is removed.
  4. At the end, pop everything left.

A + B * C becomes A B C * +. (A + B) * C becomes A B + C *.

Right-associative operators such as ^ pop only strictly greater precedence, otherwise A ^ B ^ C would associate the wrong way.

Evaluating postfix is a single stack pass: push operands, and on an operator pop two, apply, push the result.
