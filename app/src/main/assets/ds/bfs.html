Breadth-First Search

Explore all neighbours of a vertex before moving further out, using a queue.

O(V + E) with an adjacency list

BFS finds the shortest path in an unweighted graph, because it reaches every vertex by the fewest edges first. With weights, that guarantee disappears and Dijkstra is needed.

The BFS tree's level of a vertex is its distance in edges from the source. Cross edges in an undirected BFS connect vertices whose levels differ by at most one.
