Highest Response Ratio Next

Non-preemptive scheduling that balances burst length against how long a process has already waited.

Response ratio = (waiting time + burst time) / burst time

Short jobs still win, but a long job's ratio grows as it waits, so it eventually overtakes. HRRN gets much of SJF's average while avoiding its starvation.

A job with burst 10 that has waited 20 has ratio 3.0, beating a fresh job with burst 4 and ratio 1.0.
