First Come First Serve Scheduling

Run processes in arrival order, to completion. Simple, fair by arrival, and non-preemptive.

P1, P2, P3 arrive at 0 with bursts 24, 3, 3. Waiting times are 0, 24, 27 and the average is 17. Running the short ones first gives 3.

The convoy effect: one long job at the head makes every short job behind it wait, which is why FCFS has poor average waiting time.

Turnaround = completion − arrival; Waiting = turnaround − burst

FCFS never starves anyone — every process eventually reaches the head of the queue.
