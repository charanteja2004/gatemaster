Page Replacement Algorithms

On a page fault with no free frame, one resident page must be evicted. Which one decides the fault rate.

AlgorithmEvictsNote
FIFOthe oldest pagecan suffer Belady's anomaly
Optimal (OPT)the page used furthest in the futureunimplementable, used as a bound
LRUthe least recently usedgood, needs a stack or counters
Clockapproximates LRU with a reference bitwhat real systems use

Belady's anomaly: with FIFO, more frames can mean more faults. LRU and OPT are stack algorithms and are immune to it.

Reference string 1,2,3,4,1,2,5,1,2,3,4,5 gives 9 faults with three FIFO frames and 10 with four — the standard demonstration.
