Preemptive and Non-preemptive Scheduling

Non-preemptivePreemptive
CPU releasedonly on exit or blockalso on timer or higher priority
Response timepoorgood
Overheadlowcontext switches
ExamplesFCFS, SJF, LJFSRTF, Round Robin, LRTF

Preemption is what makes starvation possible for long jobs and makes shared data need locking. Both problems are absent from a non-preemptive scheduler and so is responsiveness.
