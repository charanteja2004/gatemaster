Swapping and Fragmentation

Swapping moves a whole process out to backing store and later brings it back, freeing memory for others.

InternalExternal
Whereinside an allocated blockbetween allocated blocks
Causefixed-size allocationvariable-size allocation
Curesmaller pagescompaction, or paging

Paging removes external fragmentation entirely — any free frame will do — at the cost of internal fragmentation averaging half a page per process.
