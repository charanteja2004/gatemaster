The Dining Philosophers Problem

Five philosophers, five forks, each needing both neighbouring forks to eat. The illustration of deadlock and starvation in one picture.

If every philosopher picks up the left fork first, all five hold one fork and wait forever: circular wait, and therefore deadlock.

  • Allow at most four philosophers at the table at once.
  • Pick up both forks only if both are free, in one atomic step.
  • Make one philosopher left-handed, breaking the symmetry and the cycle.

Each fix removes one of Coffman's four conditions. The asymmetric solution is the cheapest and the one usually expected.
