Producer-Consumer Problem

A producer fills a bounded buffer and a consumer empties it. The buffer must never overflow, underflow, or be touched by both at once.

// producer // consumer
wait(empty); wait(full);
wait(mutex); wait(mutex);
add item to buffer remove item
signal(mutex); signal(mutex);
signal(full); signal(empty);

Three semaphores: mutex for exclusion, empty counting free slots, full counting filled slots. empty starts at n and full at 0.

The counting semaphore must be acquired before the mutex. Taking mutex first lets a full-buffer producer sleep while holding the lock, and nothing can ever drain the buffer.
