Multitasking and Multiprogramming

TermMeans
Multiprogrammingseveral jobs in memory, CPU switches when one blocks
Multitaskingmultiprogramming plus timer-driven preemption
Multiprocessingmore than one physical CPU
Multithreadingseveral threads inside one process

Multiprogramming raises CPU utilisation; multitasking raises responsiveness. They answer different questions and are often confused in exam options.

CPU utilisation ≈ 1 − pⁿ for n processes each spending fraction p of their time waiting on I/O
