Disk Management

Disk access time = seek time + rotational latency + transfer time

  • Seek time: moving the arm to the right cylinder — the largest and most variable term.
  • Rotational latency: waiting for the sector, on average half a rotation.
  • Transfer time: reading the bits once under the head.

At 7200 rpm one rotation takes 8.33 ms, so average rotational latency is about 4.17 ms.

Because seek dominates, scheduling is about ordering requests by cylinder. That is why every disk scheduling algorithm is a rule for sweeping the arm.
