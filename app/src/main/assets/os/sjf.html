Shortest Job First

Pick the ready process with the smallest CPU burst. The preemptive form is Shortest Remaining Time First.

SJF is provably optimal for average waiting time among non-preemptive schedules. No other order of the same jobs does better.

It needs the burst length in advance, which is not knowable. Real systems estimate it with an exponential average of past bursts.

τ(n+1) = α · t(n) + (1 − α) · τ(n)

Long jobs can starve if short jobs keep arriving. Ageing — raising priority with waiting time — is the standard cure.
