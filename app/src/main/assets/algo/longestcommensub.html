Longest Common Subsequence

Find the longest sequence appearing in both strings in order, not necessarily contiguously.

L[i][j] = L[i−1][j−1] + 1 if x[i] = y[j], else max(L[i−1][j], L[i][j−1])

Θ(m · n)

LCS of "ABCBDAB" and "BDCABA" has length 4, for instance BCBA.

Subsequence is not substring. A substring must be contiguous; a subsequence need only preserve order, which is why the recurrence may skip a character on either side.
