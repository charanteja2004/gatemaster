Selection Sort

Find the minimum of the unsorted part and swap it into position. Repeat for each position in turn.

Θ(n²) in every case

Comparisons are always n(n−1)/2 regardless of the input, but swaps are at most n−1. That makes it attractive when writing is far more expensive than reading.

Selection sort is not stable in its usual array form: swapping a distant minimum into place jumps over equal keys and reverses them.
